OUR STORY

Inspired by Tradition,
Made for Today.

Since opening our doors in 2008, Transylvanian Traditions Bakery has been bringing the warmth of European baking traditions to Vancouver. Inspired by Romanian family recipes and a passion for handcrafted baking, we create authentic cakes, pastries, artisan breads, and our signature chimney cakes using quality ingredients and time-honoured techniques.

Every recipe reflects our belief that great baking begins with care, craftsmanship, and tradition. While our methods honour generations of European baking, we thoughtfully adapt our recipes using fresh, preservative-free ingredients to create desserts that are both authentic and unforgettable.

MEET THE OWNER

Keeping European Traditions Alive

The owner's love for good food has turned the Transylvanian Traditions Bakery sweet-shop into one of Vancouver's premier dessert spots. Since the bakery opened in February 2008, it has been featured in the Vancouver Sun, Canadian Immigrants as well as many local dinner tables. Along with over a dozen preservative-free european cakes, Transylvanian Traditions is one of the first places in Canada to make Kurtoskalacs, a delicious baked sweet lemon dough spiral, glazed with caramel and crushed walnuts. The bakery strives to constantly exceed customer's expectation, and many first time diners leave the pastry shop in awe, impressed by the moist cakes, fresh whip cream, and decadent chocolate creations. The traditional recipes have been slightly modified by the owner to reflect today's need for fresh ingredients and healthy choices.

From welcoming guests into the bakery each morning to preparing custom cakes for life's special occasions, every detail is guided by a love of craftsmanship and the belief that great baking has the power to bring people together.
